🥩 The Stall — What To Do

Temp plateaus at 150–170°F for hours during low & slow cooks. This is normal — collagen is converting to gelatin.

🕐 Ride It Out

Maximum bark development. Deeper smoke penetration. For brisket purists — do this.

Add 2–4 hrs to your cook window.

📦 Wrap It

Butcher paper: pushes through stall while preserving most bark texture.
Foil: fastest option, softens bark, adds moisture.

Saves 1–3 hrs of cook time.
